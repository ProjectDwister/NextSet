## Event setup

- Name: Bonus Rounds at Centre Court
- Format: Mixed Americano
- Players: 12 (6 female and 6 male)
- Rounds: 6
- Courts: Court #1, Court #2 and Court #3
- Draw: exactly the same optimized draw as the Glass Court event
- Date, start time and duration: selected by Animesh before the Firebase event is created

## Scoring and reveal flow

- There is no fixed total-points requirement.
- Standings update as soon as both scores for an individual court are entered.
- Round 2 onward reveals after all three courts in the previous round have both scores entered.
- The Full draw tab is administrator-only.
- Scores and standings update live across connected phones.

## Draw audit

- Every player plays all six rounds.
- All 36 male–female partnerships occur exactly once.
- No same-gender partnership occurs.
- No partnership repeats.
- Maximum opponent repetition is two.
- Opponent distribution is 48 pairs once, 12 pairs twice and 6 pairs never.
- Courts #1, #2 and #3 are rotated using the exact same assignment as the Glass Court draw.
