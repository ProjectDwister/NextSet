## First use

1. Open the hosted page while signed into Court Pop using the event creator's account.
2. Press **Create Glass Court event**. This writes the fixed roster and optimized six-round draw into Firebase.
3. Enter Neetul's and Arunima's Court Pop login phone numbers under **Administrator invitations**.
4. Press **Invite** and use the generated WhatsApp buttons.
5. Neetul and Arunima open the same live link, sign in using the invited phone number, and press **Accept administrator invitation**.

## Permissions and scoring

- The event creator is automatically an administrator and is not added as a player.
- Neetul becomes an administrator after accepting her phone-authenticated invitation and does not appear in the draw or standings.
- Arunima is both a player and an administrator after accepting her phone-authenticated invitation.
- All three administrators can add, edit, overwrite or clear scores.
- There is no fixed total-points requirement.
- Standings update once both team scores for a court are present.
- Round 2 onward is revealed after all three matches in the preceding round have both scores.
- The full draw tab is administrator-only.
